Lesson 2 – Where are you from?

Réaz Moussa – École Jean Hoarau

🎧 Listen

Steven = London  ·  Julie = Manchester
↘ voice goes down  ·  ↗ voice goes up

📖 Read and repeat

JHi, Steven! ↘ Nice to meet you. ↘
SNice to meet you too, Julie! ↘
JWhere are you from, Steven? ↘
SI'm from London. ↘ And you? ↗ Where are you from? ↘
JI'm from Manchester. ↘
SOh, nice! ↘ What's your nationality, Julie? ↘
JI'm British. ↘ And you? ↗ What's your nationality? ↘
SI'm British too! ↘
JWhat's your address, Steven? ↘
SI live at twelve Baker Street, in London. ↘ And you? ↗ What's your address? ↘
JI live at twenty-five Oak Road, in Manchester. ↘
SGreat! ↘ See you soon! ↘
JBye bye! ↘

✏️ Remember

Where are you from? → I'm from …
What's your nationality? → I'm … (British)
What's your address? → I live at … (street) · in … (town)
