Lesson 1 – Hello!

Réaz Moussa – École Jean Hoarau

🎧 Listen

Stephen = London  ·  Julie = Manchester
↘ voice goes down  ·  ↗ voice goes up

📖 Read and repeat

JHello! ↘
SGood morning! ↘
JHow are you? ↘
SI'm ill. How are you? ↘
JI'm very well, thank you. ↘
JWhat's your name? ↘
SMy name is Stephen. And you? ↗
SWhat's your name? ↘
JMy name is Julie. ↘
SWhere do you live, Julie? ↘
JI live in Manchester. ↘
JAnd you? Where do you live, Stephen? ↘
SI live in London. ↘
SBye bye! ↘
JSee you soon! ↘

✏️ Remember

How are you? → I'm …
What's your name? → My name is …
Where do you live? → I live in …
